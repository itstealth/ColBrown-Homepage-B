FOR PARENTS

Far from home. Never alone.

Choosing a boarding school is the biggest decision a family makes. Here is how we keep your son safe, healthy and known.

01
A House Master who lives with him
Every house has a full-time resident House Master or House Mother, with wardens round the clock.
02
A 20-bed Wellness Centre
A resident nurse, a doctor visiting daily and 24/7 medical support, with full health records.
03
Six nourishing meals a day
Nutritionist-planned, from Chota Haziri after morning PT to dinner in the dining hall.
04
Pastoral care, every hour
A dedicated pastoral team, a counselling centre and 50+ support programmes.
